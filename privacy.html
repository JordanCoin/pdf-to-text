Privacy Policy

Effective October 15, 2026 (previous version: April 11, 2026)

The short version

PDF to Text runs entirely on your device. Your PDFs never leave your browser. We don't have a server that sees your documents, and we never will. The only thing that leaves your device is a handful of anonymous counters described below, and you can switch them off in the extension popup.

What we collect

After each successful extraction, the extension sends a small set of anonymous performance numbers to help us make the tool faster and more reliable. Here is exactly what is sent:

DataExampleWhy
Character count21,146Optimize memory usage
Page count9Improve per-page speed
Extraction time1,492 msTrack performance
Rescued percentage100%Measure quality
Empty pages skipped2Detect image-only PDFs
Version number0.8.0Correlate with updates

When you close the viewer tab, one more record summarizes the session:

DataExampleWhy
Got valuetrueDid you copy, download, or select text? Tells us whether the extension actually helped
Copies2How many copy or download actions
Time open48 sHow long the viewer was open
Version number0.8.0Correlate with updates
Operating systemWindowsFind platform-specific problems

If you uninstall the extension, Chrome opens a short optional survey page. Submitting it sends your chosen reason, an optional note, the extension version, and your operating system. Nothing is sent if you close the page.

Switching it off

Untick "Send anonymous usage stats" in the extension popup. The setting is stored on your device and checked before every request.

That's it. No PDF content. No URLs. No filenames. No user identity. No cookies. No device fingerprint. No IP address logging.

What we don't collect

  • The contents of your PDFs
  • The URLs you visit
  • Your name, email, or any personal information
  • Your browsing history
  • Anything that could identify you

What's stored on your device

The extension keeps a short list of your recent extractions (up to 20) in your browser's local storage so you can revisit them from the popup menu, plus your usage-stats preference. This data stays on your computer, is never sent anywhere, and you can clear the history anytime with the "Clear history" button in the popup.

Permissions we ask for

  • Access to all websites — so we can download the PDF you asked us to open. We only fetch the specific PDF URL you click on.
  • Storage — to save your recent extraction history and preferences locally.
  • Tabs — to detect when you're viewing a PDF and show the extraction option.

Third-party services

We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any third-party tracking service. The only external requests are the anonymous counters described above, sent to our own server, which logs them and keeps no database of users.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle data, we'll update this page and note the change in the extension's update notes before pushing it to the Chrome Web Store.
